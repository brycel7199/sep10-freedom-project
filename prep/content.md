## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* Shot Designer
  * Shot Designer is able to create visual diagrams of camera placement, making it easier to carry out the director's vision
  * ![shot designer diagram](https://cdn.hollywoodcamerawork.com/images/areas/shotdesigner/cameradiagram1.jpg)
 
* Blender
  * Blender is a free software that is used for 3d model rendering and animations
  * Perks
    * Customizable
    * Video Editing Software
    * Modeling + Sculpting + Rendering
    * ![blender car model](https://media.cgtrader.com/variants/HyE8iE72cbFVtccL2cSsXZgf/64d1262c1acde2eb3beef249c4695a8ad88c958dd79db36f763bf631017addd0/untitled.png)

* Houdini
  * Houdini is a software used for VFX (visual effects)
  * It's used to manipulate and create:
    * Fluids, such as water (ocean waves), and lava
    * Debris & Explosions
    * Fire and Smoke

##### Hardware
* Led Panels
  * Led Panels are energy-conservative lighting plates
  * ![Led Panels](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTDmVHMU21ORcXeP7XhgJi_nzxp7ujhD21-aOkfp58EXw&s=10)
* 3D Printers
  * 3D Printers are used to build and create props for films, especially in Hollywood
  * ![3d printer](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRDOQQLm-ozmbe75WbeL78Y-tls4kXGbhZNyGusFdRDBw&s=10)
* Robotic Cameras
  * Robotic camera are small cameras, and are controlled with remotes, or by being pre-programmed.
  * They are great for precision shots, with panning, zoom, and tilt motors and lenses built into them
  * ![Robotic Camera](https://th.bing.com/th/id/OIP.1utIe5SyoE64VqKBJqRP-QHaE8?w=245&h=180&c=7&r=0&o=7&dpr=1.4&pid=1.7&rm=3)
