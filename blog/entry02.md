In this blog, I will be talking about my research and organization process for finding various technologies used in the film making process.

## Content

### Technology Summary
I've discovered several interesting things during this process, from robotic cameras to visual effects. One of the things that interested me the most was animatronics. Animatronics are basically mechanical machines, that are used to simulate life, such as the famous Tyrannosaurus-Rex from Jurassic Park. Another piece of technology that stood out to me was robotic cameras. Despite, their name, these do not think on their own.
